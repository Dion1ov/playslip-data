Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

PlaySlip does not collect your personal information. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The numbers you save stay on your device.

Information we collect

None. PlaySlip has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment details, and we operate no server that stores anything about you.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create in PlaySlip is saved locally on your iPhone and never leaves it. That includes:

  • Number sets you save to My Lines, and the bet type you tag them with
  • Dates you log as having played a line
  • Which games you choose to show on your home screen
  • Your daily generation count
  • A name and date of birth, only if you choose to enter them for the Personal Seed generation mode. These are used solely as input to a mathematical function that produces a repeatable set of numbers. They are not transmitted anywhere.

Deleting the app removes all of it. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Network requests the app makes

PlaySlip downloads published lottery draw results so it can show current numbers. These are ordinary read-only requests for public data. They send no information about you beyond what any web request necessarily includes, such as your device's IP address, which those services handle under their own policies.

  • New York State Open Data — published Powerball and Mega Millions results
  • Michigan Lottery published results, retrieved through our public data repository at github.com/Dion1ov/playslip-data

Purchases

PlaySlip Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase status is validated through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier generated for your device — not your name, email, or Apple ID. RevenueCat's policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

PlaySlip is not directed at children and is intended for adults of legal lottery age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Third-party advertising

There is none. PlaySlip contains no ad networks, no third-party marketing SDKs, and no cross-app tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it and the updated version appears on this page.

Contact
