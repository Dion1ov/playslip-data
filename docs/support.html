Why don't I see today's numbers yet?

PlaySlip shows results once the lottery publishes them, which is usually within minutes of the drawing. Pull down on the home screen, a game screen, or Number Lookup to check right away instead of waiting for the next automatic refresh.

The app says a number "fell boxed" but not that I won

That depends on how you bought the ticket. A Straight wager only pays when the digits come in exact order — a boxed fall doesn't pay on it. Box, Wheel and 2-Way do pay in any order. Open My Lines and tap the bet-type pill on a saved line to set how you actually play it, then check again.

How do I save a number I already play?

Open Number Lookup, enter the number, and after the results appear choose how you play it and tap Save. It lands in My Lines and gets checked against every drawing.

What does hot and cold mean?

It's a description of what has already happened — how often digits have appeared in past drawings. It is not a prediction. Every combination has exactly the same chance in every drawing, and past frequency does not change future odds.

Where do the results come from?

Published official sources: New York State Open Data for Powerball and Mega Millions, and official Michigan Lottery published results for Michigan games. Results can lag the live drawing, so always confirm any win through your state lottery.

I bought PlaySlip Pro and it isn't unlocked

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase. It restores free on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. If that doesn't do it, email us.

Do you have my data?

No. Everything you save stays on your device. There's no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
